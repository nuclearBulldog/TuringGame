Assignment No. 01Turing Game

A game about the ethical use of artificial intelligence. You play a student with a report due and an easy way out. Every choice — in the world and in the fight — asks the same question: what's the right thing to do?

Q1.

The overworld

Run and jump through a pixel world. Walk into an encounter and it becomes a turn-based battle.

Q2.

The shortcut

One move finishes the whole report instantly — by letting the AI write it. Take it, and see what the detector says. Your tutor would like a word.
